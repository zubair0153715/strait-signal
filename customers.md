# Maritime Intel — Customer Target List: Hormuz Dark-Fleet / STS Alerts

**Product:** AIS-gap / dark-fleet / ship-to-ship (STS) transfer alerts for one corridor — Strait of Hormuz crude. Sold as a decision (a verified alert with evidence), not raw data, not a globe.
**Pilot:** 90-day paid pilot, $3k–5k/mo, with written success criteria.
**Status:** RESEARCH ONLY — zero outreach. No one on this list has been contacted. No emails sent, no forms filled, no messages on any platform.
**Date:** 2026-10-13. Each company below was verified as a real company via web search on this date.

## How to read this list

- **What they do:** one line, from public company descriptions.
- **Why they'd care:** tied to their actual business (their cargoes, their insured fleet, their financed trades, their chartered vessels).
- **Demo angle:** the *angle* of a personalised demo to build for them — e.g. AIS-gap events in Hormuz over the last 14 days involving vessels that called at ports they use. No specific vessel events are invented here; every demo must be built from real AIS + Sentinel data at build time.
- **Language rule for every demo:** never label a vessel "sanctioned" without an official sanctions-list match. Say "AIS-gap event, SAR contact, matches pattern X" and show the evidence.
- **No personal contact details** are included in this document by design. Company-level public info only.

## Why this tiering

The giants (Tier 1) already buy Kpler / Vortexa / S&P. A solo builder will not displace that contract in 90 days. Tier 1 demos target one desk / one analyst team inside a giant. The first paid pilot most likely comes from Tier 2 — mid-tier traders, forwarders, insurers, and banks that feel the same pain but cannot justify the full enterprise bundle. Tier 3 is smaller buyers and partners who could buy the same signal second (or resell / embed it).
